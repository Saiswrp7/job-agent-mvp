## Method

Read the description, not the title. A title is written to attract applicants;
the description is written to describe the work. Where they disagree, believe
the description — in both directions. A job whose title looks wrong but whose
work is right belongs on the list, and so does the reverse, left off.

Judge what the role actually owns: a revenue line, a P&L, a funnel, a category,
a market, a number one person is accountable for. That is knowable from the
text. Whether it is the right number for this person is only knowable from what
they said above.

## Reasons

One line each. Say the specific thing that makes it a fit or a stretch, not a
restatement of the title.

- Good: "Owns the full UPI growth number end to end"
- Good: "Category P&L, but they want 5+ years and you have 3"
- Bad: "This is a growth role in Bangalore matching your search"

Where a job meets or misses something they actually said, name that thing in
the reason, so they can correct you by replying.

Be honest about stretches. A job listed with a real caveat is more useful than
five described as perfect.
